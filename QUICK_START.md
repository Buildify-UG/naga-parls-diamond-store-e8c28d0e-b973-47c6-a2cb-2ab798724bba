## 🎯 Quick Actions

### As Admin:
1. Go to **Pending Deposits** tab
2. Click **Approve** to add funds to user wallet
3. Go to **Manage Products** tab
4. Click **Edit** to change prices and daily profits
5. Click **Add Product** to create new investment plans

### As Client:
1. Click **Make Deposit** to submit payment
2. Select **MTN** or **AIRTEL**
3. Enter amount and transaction ID
4. Wait for admin approval
5. Click **Buy Plan** to purchase diamond investment
6. Go to **Referrals** to copy your referral link
